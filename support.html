SIZE & CARE

What shirt is the art printed on?

The collection is printed on demand on the Gildan 5000 Heavy Cotton tee: a classic, slightly boxy unisex fit in a mid-weight fabric (about 5.0–5.3 oz/yd², 170–180 g/m²). It is 100% cotton, with a cotton-polyester blend in some heather colors, and is knitted in a tube so there are no side seams.

Each shirt is printed when you order it, so nothing sits in a warehouse. Sizes typically run S to 2XL; the exact sizes and colors for each design are shown in its preview.

What else are the designs printed on?

Most designs are also made as a crewneck sweatshirt and a hoodie (sizes S to 5XL), a canvas tote, kiss-cut stickers in several sizes (including a bumper sticker), and an apron. Each design’s preview shows the products, colors and sizes it comes in. Like the shirts, everything is made when you order it.

How do I choose a size?

It’s a unisex, men’s-cut tee. If you’re between sizes or prefer a closer fit, compare with a classic cotton tee you already own. For the exact chest and length measurements of a size, email Nino with the design name and he’ll send the chart.

How should I care for my shirt?

To keep the print bright: turn the shirt inside out, machine wash cold with similar colors, and tumble dry low or hang to dry. Don’t iron directly on the print and skip bleach. Always check the garment’s care label too.

DELIVERY & CHECKOUT

Where does it ship, and what will it cost?

When ordering is open, available destinations and delivery options are shown during checkout. Review your delivery address, shipping charge, applicable taxes, and final total before paying.

Every shirt is printed when you order it. Printing usually takes 2–5 business days before it ships, and you’ll get an email with tracking when it’s on its way. Delivery options and prices for your address are shown at checkout before you pay. For a gift or a firm date, email Nino before ordering.

Orders shipped outside the U.S. may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country.

Is there a free sticker?

Yes. Every order placed on this website comes with a free 3-inch sticker of the design you ordered (the first design, if you order more than one). It’s added to your order automatically and ships with it.

Why is an option or checkout unavailable?

A design can be visible while its purchasable options are unavailable. Choose another available option or contact Nino for help. If checkout is not open, payment cannot be completed; your cart can stay saved in this browser when browser storage is available.

Do I need an Etsy account?

No. Browse and use the cart here. When checkout is available, you can purchase through this website without an Etsy account.

ORDER HELP

My order is damaged, incorrect, or missing. What should I send?

Email hello@ninobattaglia.com with your order reference, the design name, and a description of the issue. For a damaged or incorrect item, include clear photos of the item and packaging if available. Never email payment card details.

Ask for instructions before sending anything back. Nino can review the issue and explain the next steps.

Returns & exchanges

Because each item is made to order, we can’t accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind or the wrong size, so please check sizing before ordering.

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn’t what you ordered, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We’ll send a replacement or a refund. Never send anything back without contacting us first.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes — email us as soon as possible. We can cancel for a full refund until it goes into printing, usually within a few hours of ordering.

YOUR INFORMATION

  • We use your name, address, and email only to make, ship, and support your order.
  • Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Your address and order details are shared with our print partner, Printful, to make and ship your order.
  • If you sign up for news, we keep your email address only to send it. Every news email has an unsubscribe link, or email Nino to be taken off the list.
  • We don’t sell your information or use advertising trackers. To count visits we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn’t identify or follow individual visitors.

The cart uses this browser’s local storage to remember selected product options and quantities. Checkout recovery uses session storage in the current browser tab. If storage is blocked, some cart or checkout features may not work.

When you request delivery rates or purchase, the order service processes the information needed for shipping, payment verification, and fulfillment. Never include card details in support emails. Contact Nino about your information.

Optional shopping measurement

Shopping measurement is off unless you turn it on below. When enabled, this site sends counts of page views, product views, additions to cart, checkout attempts, and checkout openings to its own order service. It does not send search terms, names, email addresses, delivery addresses, cart contents, receipt tokens, or payment details as measurement data. Product views and additions may include a product ID.

Daily action totals use a 90-day window; expired totals are removed at the next scheduled cleanup. There are no measurement cookies or visitor identifiers. Counts cannot identify unique visitors or show which steps belonged to the same person. Hosting providers still process connection information to serve requests. Confirmed purchases are counted separately from verified order records, excluding test payments.

You can turn this off here at any time. A browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal also disables it. Your preference is stored on this device.
