MY HAPPY
DONUT

Start a project like this ↗

A sidewalk sign for a neighborhood donut shop, made with Menu-Made.

The brief

  • A sturdy A-frame sign for outside the shop
  • One side promoting breakfast
  • One side promoting lunch

The result

Two bold, menu-style faces built around the shop’s cheerful character art — a breakfast side and an “Amazing Lunch” side — so passers-by see what’s on, whichever way they’re walking.
